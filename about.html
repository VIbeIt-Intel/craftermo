About

For the love of wood.

Crafter Mo — Mohammed Steyn — designs and builds custom wooden furniture from his workshop in Tzaneen, Limpopo.

The maker

Hands on the timber

Mo works as designer and artisan under Crafter Mo / Wood Crafters — custom exotic wood pieces for homes, lodges, and anyone who wants furniture with real grain and weight.

The crest, the burned CM mark, and the workshop benches all say the same thing: quality forever, built by someone who still loves the shavings on the floor.

As long as it's wood, it's worth it.
