Mohammed Steyn

They call him Crafter Mo.

Mohammed Steyn is Crafter Mo. He designs and builds custom wooden furniture, and he takes on work right across South Africa. The workshops are in Tzaneen, Limpopo, and in Margate on the South Coast.

Wood Crafter's

Still the one on the tools.

Mo still makes the pieces himself. You bring him a room, a sketch, or a slab of timber, and he will tell you what he can do with it.

The work goes out as Crafter Mo and Wood Crafter's. He is still the one on the tools, and the CM mark still gets burned into the timber before it leaves.

He works with the grain in front of him, keeps a live edge when it belongs, and puts old silky oak back to work. The benches never really get cleared.

As long as it's wood, it's worth it.
